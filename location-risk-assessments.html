WHY IT MATTERS

The Right Location Protects Young People

Ofsted requires that every supported accommodation property is suitable for the young people who will live there. A poor location assessment — or none at all — is one of the most common reasons registration applications are delayed or refused.

Our consultants conduct a thorough on-site and contextual analysis of each property, covering environmental risks, proximity to harmful influences, community resources, transport links, and the specific needs of young people aged 16–17 in your care.
