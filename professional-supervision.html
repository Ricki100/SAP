WHY IT MATTERS

Supervision Is a Regulatory Requirement - Not Optional

The Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023 require that all staff receive regular, structured supervision. Ofsted inspectors will review your supervision records and challenge managers on the depth and quality of conversations being held.

Poor or absent supervision is a direct risk factor for safeguarding failures and negative Ofsted outcomes. Our consultants can provide external supervision for Registered Managers, and help you build a robust internal supervision framework for all staff.
