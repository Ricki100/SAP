WHO IT IS FOR

Are you new to managing an Ofsted-registered supported accommodation service?

Our practical 12-week mentoring programme supports Registered Service Managers and Nominated Individuals to develop the knowledge, confidence, and leadership skills needed to run an effective, safe, and well-led service.

Through weekly one-hour sessions, you receive focused guidance, constructive challenge, and practical tools that can be applied directly within your organisation.

HOW IT WORKS

A focused 12-week pathway for confident leadership

Each weekly session is practical, reflective, and action-focused. You leave with a clearer understanding of your role, what good looks like, and what to do next inside your service.
