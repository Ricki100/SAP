WHY IT MATTERS

The Right Tools Drive the Right Culture

Generic, off-the-shelf templates are one of the most common sources of poor recording in supported accommodation. When staff are given tools that don't fit the work, the recording suffers — and so does the Ofsted outcome.

We design bespoke, professionally structured templates that embed the language, prompts, and outcome-focus Ofsted inspectors expect to see. Every template is tested against the Supported Accommodation Regulations 2023 and the SCCIF Quality Standards before delivery.
