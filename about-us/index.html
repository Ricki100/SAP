We are a specialist consultancy dedicated to helping supported accommodation providers achieve regulatory compliance, improve service quality, and deliver outstanding outcomes for the young people in their care.

Who We Are

Built on Experience.
Driven by Purpose.

For over a decade, Supported Accommodation Providers Consultancy has stood at the forefront of the social care sector, navigating the complex intersection of operational excellence and regulatory compliance.

Our work began with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between policy and practice for supported accommodation providers. With 10+ years of sector experience, we help services strengthen compliance, quality assurance, leadership and the evidence they need to demonstrate progress.

We do not just provide advice; we help providers understand risk, prioritise action and build practical systems that stand up to scrutiny. Our consultants work within the evolving landscape of the Supported Accommodation Regulations 2023.

Taps Kundoro

Chief Consultant

Ofsted Expert Operational Lead Strategic Advisor

"Taps Kundoro (BA (Hons) Youth and Community Work, Master of Business Administration) is an experienced Ofsted Registered Manager and Nominated Individual with extensive knowledge of the supported accommodation sector. Taps has significant experience in establishing and registering Ofsted-regulated Supported Accommodation provisions and has successfully supported services through Ofsted inspections, monitoring visits, and regulatory compliance processes. With a strong understanding of operational leadership, quality assurance, and regulatory requirements, Taps provides practical, outcome-focused support to organisations across the sector, helping providers strengthen compliance, improve service quality, and achieve positive outcomes for young people."
