WHO WE ARE

Built on Experience.
Driven by Purpose.

Since the introduction of new regulations for supported accommodation for young people aged 16 and 17, providers must now be registered with Ofsted. This registration represents a significant shift in the operational landscape of the sector.

The registration process is rigorous, requiring detailed documentation, compliance with frameworks, and clear evidence of financial viability. Our application is not only compliant but represents the highest standards of care and support.
