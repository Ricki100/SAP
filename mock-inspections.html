WHO WE ARE

See The Service Behind The Paperwork.

A mock inspection is a comprehensive, objective assessment of your supported accommodation service. It is designed to simulate the experience of a real Ofsted inspection, providing you with a critical baseline of your current regulatory standing.

Our consultants spend time with your team, review your documentation, and look at how leadership, safeguarding, support planning, records, risk management and outcomes connect in practice. This gives you a practical baseline before external scrutiny arrives.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

A typical mock inspection takes 1 to 2 full days on-site, depending on the size and number of locations within your service. This ensures we have enough time to speak with staff and residents as well as review service documentation.

Mock inspections are carried out by professionals with specialist supported accommodation experience and detailed knowledge of the Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023 and SCCIF expectations.

A lower-scoring mock inspection is actually the most valuable outcome - it gives you a clear, prioritised roadmap for improvement before the real inspection. We will work alongside you to address every gap identified in the report.
